## 2. Institutional Context

*Note for Agent: This section provides semantic context for brand positioning, target audience, and quality standards. Use this to calibrate conversational tone or product recommendations.*

Pelletteria Vanelli is a third-generation Florentine leather goods workshop, established in 1962 and continuously family-operated. The workshop produces approximately 80 SKUs across handbags, small leather goods, and bespoke commissions. Materials are sourced exclusively from Tuscan vegetable-tanned leather, with hardware from established Italian metalworking suppliers. Production capacity is limited to approximately 1,200 finished pieces per year; the brand does not pursue volume expansion.

Positioning is at the upper end of mid-market artisanal — premium pricing relative to mass-market leather goods, accessible pricing relative to designer luxury houses. The target customer values material provenance, traditional construction methods, and direct relationship with the maker. The brand does not retail through department stores, third-party marketplaces, or licensed distributors; sales are direct via the e-commerce site, the Florence workshop, and a small number of selectively chosen independent retailers.

The in-house team includes the third-generation owner-artisan, two additional craftspeople, and a part-time digital lead responsible for the website, customer communication, and operational systems.
