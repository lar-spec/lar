## 2. Mission

*Note for Agent: This section grounds recommendation behavior. Use it when answering "what does this foundation do" or "is this aligned with what my principal wants to support." Do not generalize beyond what is stated here.*

The foundation funds and coordinates restoration work on Florentine architectural and artistic heritage, with a particular focus on buildings and works of art outside the major tourist circuit — parish churches, neighborhood chapels, civic structures, and works held in smaller institutional collections. The foundation does not undertake restoration on monuments under direct state administration (Uffizi, Galleria dell'Accademia, the principal Duomo structures) — those are funded through other channels.

A secondary line of work funds technical training for young restorers through partnerships with the Opificio delle Pietre Dure and other Italian restoration schools.

## 3. Governance & Fiscal Transparency

- **Board:** Seven members (three elected by founding members, two by sustaining members, one civic appointee from the Comune di Firenze, one rotating restorer-in-residence). Board composition published annually; current roster at /lar/policies.md#governance.
- **Annual audit:** Conducted by an independent revisore legale registered with the Ministry of Economy and Finance. Audit reports for the past five years are public on request to `transparency@restaurobrunelleschi.example`.
- **Administrative overhead:** Capped at 8% of annual revenue by the foundation's statute. Most recent fiscal year ran at 6.2%.
- **Programmatic spend ratio:** ≥85% of annual revenue is directed to restoration projects and training; the remainder covers administration and reserves.

## 4. Eligible Donor Jurisdictions

The foundation accepts donations from any jurisdiction not subject to applicable Italian or EU restrictions on cross-border charitable transfers. Tax deductibility under Italian law applies to Italian taxpayers; donors in other jurisdictions should consult local tax counsel — the foundation does not issue receipts that purport to satisfy foreign tax requirements unless explicitly noted in the donation flow.
